# Contacting support

Our support team answers chat messages every day.

## What support can do

Support can look up your orders and account, cancel or change pending orders, return or exchange delivered items,
update your default address, and answer questions about products and policies.

## Talking to a person

Our assistant hands your conversation to a person when you ask for one, or when your request is outside what it can
do. Some refunds and exceptions also need a person's approval before they go through.

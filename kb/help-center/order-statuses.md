# What order statuses mean

Your order's status tells you what can still be done with it.

## Statuses

- **pending**: placed but not yet processed. It can be cancelled or changed.
- **pending (item modified)**: item options were changed once. It can no longer be changed or cancelled.
- **processed**: packed and on its way. It can no longer be cancelled or changed.
- **delivered**: arrived. Items can be returned or exchanged.
- **cancelled**: cancelled and refunded.
- **return requested**: a return was started; watch your email for instructions.
- **exchange requested**: an exchange was started; watch your email for instructions.

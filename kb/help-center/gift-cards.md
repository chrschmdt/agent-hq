# Using gift cards

Gift cards on your account work like any other payment method, with a balance.

## Paying with a gift card

A gift card can pay for an order only if its balance covers the whole amount. The same applies when you use it to pay
a price difference after modifying or exchanging items.

## Refunds to a gift card

Refunds to a gift card are added to its balance immediately, which makes it the fastest way to get your money back.
A return can always be refunded to a gift card already on your account.

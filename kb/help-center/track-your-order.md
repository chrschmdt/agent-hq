# Tracking your order

Every order that has shipped has at least one tracking number.

## Where to find tracking

Support can look up your order by its order number, which starts with **#W**, for example #W1234567. Each shipment
lists its tracking number and the items inside it. Orders that are still **pending** have not shipped yet, so they
have no tracking number.

## When to expect delivery

Delivery time depends on the carrier. See **Carriers and transit times** for the usual number of days, and
**Delivery delays** if your parcel is late.

# Changing an address

There are two different addresses: the default address on your account, and the shipping address of each order.

## Your account's default address

Support can update the default address on your account at any time. New orders use it, but orders you already placed
keep their own shipping address.

## An order's shipping address

The shipping address of an order can be changed only while the order is **pending**. Once it has been processed, the
parcel is on its way and the address cannot be changed.

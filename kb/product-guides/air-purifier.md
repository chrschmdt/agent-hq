# Air Purifier guide

Choose an Air Purifier by room size and filter type.

## Room size

Models are rated for **small**, **medium** or **large** rooms. A purifier rated for a smaller room will clean a
larger room slowly.

## Filters and features

Filter types are **HEPA** (fine particles and allergens), **carbon** (odors) and **ionic**. Features include
**quiet operation**, **night mode** and **smart sensors** that adjust fan speed to the air quality.

# Espresso Machine guide

Our Espresso Machine comes in three types and several sizes.

## Types

- **Automatic:** grinds, doses and brews at the press of a button.
- **Manual:** you control dose and extraction yourself.
- **Capsule:** brews from pre-filled capsules.

## Pressure and capacity

Pump pressure is **9**, **15** or **19 bar**, and water tank capacity is **1L**, **1.5L** or **2L**.

## Care

Descale every one to three months depending on your water hardness, and rinse the portafilter after each use.

# Smart Thermostat guide

The Smart Thermostat learns your schedule and can be controlled from your phone or a smart home system.

## Compatibility

Each thermostat works with one smart home platform, chosen when you buy it:

- **Apple HomeKit**, controlled from the Apple Home app and Siri
- **Google Assistant**, controlled from the Google Home app and Google Nest speakers
- **Amazon Alexa**, controlled from the Alexa app and Echo speakers

A thermostat for Google Assistant is the one to choose for Google Home. A thermostat for one platform does not work
with the others; to switch platforms, exchange it for the same thermostat with the other compatibility.

## Colors

Available in **black**, **white** and **stainless steel**.

# Wireless Earbuds guide

Our Wireless Earbuds come in versions with different battery life and water resistance.

## Battery life

Versions offer **4**, **6** or **8 hours** of playback on a single charge.

## Water resistance

- **IPX7:** survives brief immersion in water.
- **IPX4:** resists splashes and sweat.
- **Not resistant:** keep away from water.

Colors are **black**, **white** and **blue**.

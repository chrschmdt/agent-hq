# Bluetooth Speaker guide

The Bluetooth Speaker pairs with any phone, tablet or laptop that supports Bluetooth.

## Battery and water resistance

Versions offer **10** or **20 hours** of battery life, and come either **water resistant** or not.

## Colors

Available in **black**, **blue**, **green** and **red**.

## Pairing tips

If the speaker does not appear on your device, turn it off and on again, and remove it from your device's Bluetooth
list before pairing again.

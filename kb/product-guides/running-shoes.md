# Running Shoes guide

Our Running Shoes come in a range of sizes, colors and materials.

## Sizing

Sizes are **8**, **9** and **10** in whole sizes only. If you are between sizes, most runners choose the larger size.
A different size is an exchange for the same product, not a new order.

## Materials and soles

Uppers are **mesh**, **synthetic** or **leather**. Soles are **EVA** foam for cushioning or **rubber** for grip.

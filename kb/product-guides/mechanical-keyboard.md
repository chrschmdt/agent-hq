# Mechanical Keyboard guide

Our Mechanical Keyboard comes in several combinations of switch type, backlight and size.

## Switch types

- **Clicky:** a tactile bump and an audible click on every press.
- **Tactile:** a bump you can feel, without the click.
- **Linear:** smooth and quiet from top to bottom.

## Backlight and size

Backlight options are **RGB**, **white** or **none**. Sizes are **full size** (with number pad), **80%** (no number
pad) and **60%** (no number pad or arrow keys). Not every combination is always in stock.

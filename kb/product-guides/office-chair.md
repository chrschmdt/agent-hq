# Office Chair guide

The Office Chair comes in several materials, colors and support options.

## Materials and colors

Seats are **mesh**, **fabric** or **leather**, in **black**, **gray**, **blue** or **red**.

## Support options

Armrests are **adjustable**, **fixed** or absent (**none**). The backrest is **standard** or **high-back**; a
high-back supports the upper back and shoulders.

# Handing a conversation to a person

Hand a conversation to a person only when it is needed.

## When to hand off

- The customer explicitly asks for a person.
- The request cannot be handled with the available actions and policy.

## How to hand off

Transfer the conversation with a short summary: who the customer is, what they asked for, what was already checked or
done, and why it needs a person. Then tell the customer they are being transferred.

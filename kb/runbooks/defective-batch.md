# Handling a defective batch

Use this runbook when complaints cluster around one product variant.

## Detect

Look for tickets and low reviews that mention the same problem for the same item ID within a few days. Similar
complaint text and a shared variant are stronger evidence than a rise in volume alone.

## Respond

1. Collect the ticket and review IDs as evidence.
2. Propose pausing sales of the affected variant, and a help center article describing the problem and the options.
3. Keep offering returns and exchanges under the normal policy while the proposal is reviewed.

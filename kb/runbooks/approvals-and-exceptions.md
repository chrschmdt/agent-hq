# Approvals and policy exceptions

Some actions need a person's approval before they go through.

## What needs approval

- Any refund over **$100**: the refund of a return, or a price difference paid back after an exchange or an item change.
- Any exception to written policy, such as a return reason or window the policy does not allow.

## How to request approval

Pause the action and send it for approval with the order, the amount, the policy it relies on, and why an exception
is justified. Do not tell the customer the action is done until it is approved.

## After a decision

If approved, carry out the action exactly as approved. If rejected, explain the outcome to the customer using the
reason given, and offer what the policy does allow.

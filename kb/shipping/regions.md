# Shipping regions

Operations groups deliveries into regions to track carrier performance.

## Regions

- **Northeast:** New York, Pennsylvania, District of Columbia.
- **Southeast:** Florida, North Carolina.
- **Midwest:** Ohio, Indiana, Illinois.
- **Texas:** Texas.
- **Mountain:** Colorado, Arizona.
- **West:** California, Washington.

## How regions are used

Daily KPIs report deliveries and late delivery rates by carrier and by region. A spike for one carrier in one region
usually points to a local carrier problem rather than a store problem.

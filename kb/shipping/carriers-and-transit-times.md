# Carriers and transit times

We ship with three carriers. The carrier is chosen when your parcel ships.

## Transit times

- **Swiftline:** 2 to 3 days after shipping.
- **Parcelway:** 3 to 5 days after shipping.
- **Northstar Post:** 4 to 7 days after shipping.

The promised delivery date is the last day of that range, counted from the day the parcel ships.

## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States.

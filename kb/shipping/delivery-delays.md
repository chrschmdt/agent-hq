# Delivery delays

Most parcels arrive on time, but weather, volume and carrier problems can cause delays.

## If your parcel is late

A parcel is late once its promised delivery date has passed. Late parcels usually arrive within one to three days.
Support can check the shipment's status and tell you which carrier has it.

## Proactive notices

When we see delays affecting one carrier or region, we may contact affected customers before they ask.

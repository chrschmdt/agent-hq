# Refunds and payment methods

We accept three kinds of payment method: **credit cards**, **PayPal** and **gift cards**.

## How long refunds take

- **Gift cards:** refunds are added to the card balance immediately.
- **Credit cards and PayPal:** refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear.

## Gift card balances

A gift card can pay for an order, a price difference, or a changed payment only if its balance covers the full
amount. Gift card balances cannot be withdrawn as cash.

## Price differences

When items in an order are modified or exchanged, the difference between the old and new prices is charged to, or
refunded to, the payment method you choose for that change.

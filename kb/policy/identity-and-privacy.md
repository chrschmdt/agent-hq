# Verifying your identity

To protect your account, support verifies who you are before looking at or changing anything.

## How we verify you

We find your account with the **email address** on it, or with your **first name, last name and zip code**. This
happens at the start of every conversation, even if you already know your user ID.

## One account per conversation

A support conversation covers one customer's account. We can handle several requests for that account, but we
cannot discuss or change another customer's orders or details in the same conversation.

## Confirming changes

Before we cancel, change, return or exchange anything, we list exactly what will happen and ask you to confirm with
a clear yes.

# Changing a pending order

While an order is **pending**, you can change three things: the shipping address, the payment method, and the options
of the items you ordered. Nothing else about an order can be changed.

## Shipping address

The address of a pending order can be updated to any address you give us. Changing an order's address does not change
the default address on your account; that is a separate update.

## Payment method

You can move the payment to a single payment method that is different from the one used. If the new method is a gift
card, its balance must cover the full order total. The order stays **pending**. The original payment is refunded
immediately if it was a gift card, otherwise within 5 to 7 business days.

## Item options

Each item can be switched to an available item of the **same product** with different options, for example a
different color or size. You cannot switch to a different product, such as a shirt for shoes. You choose a payment
method to pay or receive the price difference; a gift card must cover any amount due.

Item options can be changed **only once** per order, so collect every item you want to change before confirming. The
status then becomes **pending (item modified)**, and the order cannot be modified or cancelled after that.

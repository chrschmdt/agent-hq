# Returning items

Items from a delivered order can be returned for a refund.

## Which orders can be returned

An order can be returned only when its status is **delivered**. You choose which items to return; they do not all
have to be from the same product.

## Where the refund goes

The refund goes either to the **original payment method** of the order or to a **gift card** already on your
account. It cannot go to a different credit card or PayPal account.

## What happens next

After you confirm the order and the items, the order status changes to **return requested**, and you receive an
email explaining how and where to send the items back. A delivered order can be returned or exchanged once.
